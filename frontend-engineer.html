Role fit React · Next.js · TypeScript

Frontend Engineer.

I have less than 5 years of work experience, and most of it is React, Next.js and TypeScript: in production, on a team, with real users on the other end. This is the role I am strongest in. Here is why, with the evidence next to each claim.

Why I fit

  1. I have already done this job, in production

    At Artco Group I built and maintained production web applications in React, Next.js and TypeScript in an Agile/Scrum team. Interfaces, API integration, authentication guards on protected routes, real-time dashboards: the everyday work of a frontend engineer on a product people rely on.

  2. I know the framework below its API

    Most frontend bugs live where the abstraction leaks. At Artco Group I diagnosed and resolved SSR hydration issues in production Next.js, the kind of bug you only fix once you know what the server sent and what the client expected.

    In Online Ticket Booth I used Next.js 16’s Cache Components and Partial Prerendering where each is the right answer, and I can explain where I refused to. artifact A-04

  3. Performance and accessibility are part of the build

    ÉTHER puts 26,000 moving particles behind a headline while keeping the text server-rendered, the canvas paused when offscreen, and the motion optional for people who ask for less. artifact A-06

    CO2 Footprint comes at performance from the other side. It reads the browser’s own Performance API and turns the weight of any page into grams of CO2 per visit, so page weight is a number I can point at, not a feeling. artifact A-02

  4. I care how an interface feels, not only that it works

    Drift is an experiment in bringing filters to the cursor: a radial menu that knows what you clicked, with undo on every change and a keyboard path for everything. artifact A-07

  5. I test what I ship, and I make teams better

    At work I wrote unit and integration suites with Vitest and Jest and led code reviews. As team lead in 2024 I coordinated deliverables, and I mentored two interns from fundamentals to production-ready work. One of them was hired full-time.

What I am looking for

A hands-on frontend role on a product team that cares about craft. Somewhere I can ship maintainable interfaces, keep learning from people ahead of me, and help the people coming up behind me.

I am based in Zenica, Bosnia and Herzegovina, open to remote work, and available now.
