Product Engineer.

The part of engineering I like most is the whole of it: understanding what someone actually needs, deciding what to build, shipping it, and staying around to see whether it worked.

Why I fit

  1. I talk to the people who use the thing

    On the membership registry I was the only developer, so the requirements came to me directly from a non-technical client. I turned them into a data model and a set of screens, and stayed with the system in production afterwards. artifact A-01

  2. I ship products, not only features

    The Advanced Calculator is mine from the domain name down. I designed it, built it, deployed it and did the search optimization, and I have put 39 commits into it since April 2025. Nobody assigned that work. artifact A-03

  3. I make the default decision, then defend it

    Product engineering is mostly judgment calls. In the ticket booth the same number is cached for a minute in one place and never cached in another, because the cost of being wrong differs. In the registry, permissions became a settings screen because policy changes more often than code should. artifact A-04

  4. I sweat how it feels

    Drift started from a product question, not a technical one: what if filters came to the cursor? The answer had to stay predictable, so every change is reversible and reachable from the keyboard. artifact A-07

  5. I measure instead of guessing

    My diagram agent is graded by eight scorers over a 30-case golden dataset, so a change has to prove it is an improvement. I bring the same habit to AI features in a product: evals first, opinions second. artifact A-05 More on that in How I Build.

  6. I can carry a team’s delivery

    As team lead at Artco Group in 2024 I coordinated deliverables while still shipping, and I mentored two interns into production-ready developers.

What I am looking for

A small product team where engineers are trusted with outcomes, not handed tickets. I want to be close to users, own a problem end to end, and be judged on whether it got solved.

I am based in Zenica, Bosnia and Herzegovina, open to remote work, and available now.
